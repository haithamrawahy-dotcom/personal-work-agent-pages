Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Personal Work Agent is a personal productivity application currently intended for use by its owner.

Information Accessed

With Google account authorization, the application accesses Gmail messages and Google Calendar events to help organize work, identify important changes, and prepare summaries.

How Information Is Used

Information is processed to answer the user's requests, generate productivity summaries, and support authorized calendar actions. Selected information may be processed through an AI service to generate responses and summaries.

Data Storage

The application stores task records, operational checkpoints, generated summaries, and authentication credentials in its configured storage environment. Email and calendar information may appear in generated summaries.

Data Sharing

Google account data is not sold or used for advertising. Data may be transmitted to service providers required to operate the application, including AI processing and hosting providers.

Access and Revocation

Google account access can be revoked through the user's Google Account security settings. Revoking access prevents further authorized retrieval but does not automatically delete previously stored information.

Security

The application uses access controls and configuration safeguards intended to protect credentials and stored information.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the support email displayed on the Google authorization screen.
